## Current Focus

<table>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" valign="top">

<h3>🧪 PhD Project · AI for Polymer Design</h3>

<p><strong>Our research group</strong> focuses on polymer design and has <strong>wet-lab capabilities for experimental validation</strong>. My role is the <strong>machine-learning and deep-learning component</strong>.</p>

<p>I develop domain foundation models through corpus curation, sequence–graph representation learning, and <strong>pretraining, continued pretraining, and post-training</strong>. Small-molecule-to-polymer transfer provides the setting for studying how chemical knowledge can be encoded and reused.</p>

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" valign="top">

<h3>🧠 LLM Post-Training &amp; Test-Time Training</h3>

<p>I develop and evaluate methods for <strong>general-purpose and scientific LLMs</strong>, including <strong>SFT, knowledge distillation, PEFT, and preference optimization</strong>. My work includes LoRA, DoRA, soft prompts, DPO, synthetic supervision, and preference-data construction.</p>

<p>My <strong>test-time training</strong> research covers per-instance and shared updates for long-context reasoning. I study <strong>temporary parametric memory</strong>, self-supervised objectives, selective updates, state isolation, and rollback, with explicit evaluation of <strong>quality, latency, and GPU cost</strong>.</p>

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

<h3>💾 LLM Memory &amp; Retrieval</h3>

<p>I study <strong>external and parametric memory</strong>, session/episodic memory, and <strong>RAG</strong>: what to store, when to retrieve or update it, and how long to retain it. This connects retrieval-guided TTT with long-context reasoning and runtime routing.</p>

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

<h3>🤖 Multi-Agent Systems &amp; Harnesses</h3>

<p>I study <strong>multi-agent coordination and agent harnesses</strong> for planning, tool use, state management, verification, and recovery. My GUI-agent research explores <strong>protocol-state planning and failure recovery</strong> across interfaces, alongside broader work on stateful agent workflows.</p>

</td>
</tr>
</table>
