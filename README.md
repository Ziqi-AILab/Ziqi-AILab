I am a PhD student working at the intersection of cheminformatics and large language models. My research investigates how LLMs acquire, update, retain, retrieve, and apply scientific knowledge across their lifecycle—from domain pretraining and post-training to memory, runtime adaptation, and agentic execution.

## Current Focus

- **LLM Post-Training** — Knowledge distillation, parameter-efficient fine-tuning with LoRA and DoRA, preference optimization, and controlled adaptation of foundation models under limited data and compute.

- **Molecular & Polymer Foundation Models** — Investigating how chemical knowledge can be encoded into language models through domain corpus curation, tokenizer–structure alignment, equivalent sequence views, sequence–graph pretraining, continued pretraining, distillation, preference optimization, and structured memory.

- **Memory & Runtime Adaptation** — Retrieval-augmented generation, parametric and session memory, test-time adaptation, test-time scaling, and runtime routing for models that respond effectively to changing tasks and context.

- **LLM Agents** — Tool use, GUI interaction, stateful execution, and failure recovery for agents that maintain context, coordinate multi-step actions, and support complex scientific workflows.
