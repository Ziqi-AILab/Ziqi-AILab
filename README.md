I am a PhD student in cheminformatics, developing language-model-based foundation models for molecular and polymer science. My doctoral research studies how chemical knowledge can be encoded, transferred, retained, and updated across the model lifecycle—from pretraining on small-molecule sequence–structure data and molecule-to-polymer continued pretraining to polymer-domain post-training and memory-based adaptation.

Alongside this domain research, I pursue a broader, method-centered research line in LLM post-training and runtime adaptation. My work covers knowledge distillation, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, preference optimization, test-time adaptation and scaling, retrieval and memory, and agentic systems.

## Current Focus

- **Cheminformatics & Small-Molecule-to-Polymer Foundation Models** — Developing language-model-based scientific foundation models across small molecules and polymers, spanning domain corpus construction, tokenizer–structure alignment, small-molecule pretraining, molecule-to-polymer continued pretraining, polymer-domain post-training, and memory-based adaptation. A central question is how chemical knowledge can be encoded into foundation models and transferred reliably from molecular to polymer domains.

- **LLM Post-Training** — Developing and evaluating post-training methods for both general-purpose and scientific LLMs, including knowledge distillation, supervised adaptation, parameter-efficient fine-tuning with LoRA and DoRA, preference optimization, synthetic supervision, and controlled model updates under limited data and compute.

- **Memory & Runtime Adaptation** — Retrieval-augmented generation, parametric and session memory, test-time training, test-time scaling, and runtime routing, with a focus on deciding when a model should read, retrieve, reason, update, or act.

- **LLM Agents & Harnesses** — Tool use, GUI interaction, stateful execution, verification, and failure recovery for agents that maintain context, coordinate multi-step actions, and operate reliably across complex workflows.
